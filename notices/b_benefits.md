Subject: Good news: more ways to earn with your Meridian card

Starting March 9, your Meridian card comes with new Harbor Bank perks: up to 10% cash back at hundreds of online stores through Harbor Offers, and 5% back on hotels and rental cars booked through Harbor Travel. Your current rewards rate and balance stay exactly as they are.

To start using these perks, set up your Harbor Bank login. Open the Meridian app and follow the prompt. It takes about five minutes.

After March 9 you will manage your card on the Harbor Bank website and app, and your Meridian login will stop working. Your card number, credit limit, and scheduled payments do not change.
