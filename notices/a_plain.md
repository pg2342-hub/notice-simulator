Subject: Your Meridian card account is moving to Harbor Bank

Harbor Bank acquired Meridian, and your Meridian credit card account will move to Harbor Bank on March 9.

What stays the same: your card number, your rewards balance, your credit limit, and your scheduled payments.

What changes: after March 9 you will manage your card on the Harbor Bank website and app. You will need to create a Harbor Bank login the first time you sign in. Your Meridian login will stop working on that date.

To get ready, open the Meridian app now and follow the prompt to set up your Harbor Bank login. It takes about five minutes.
