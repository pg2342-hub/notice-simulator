Subject: Your Meridian card is moving to Harbor Bank. Here is how to know this message is real.

Your Meridian credit card account moves to Harbor Bank on March 9. Your card number, rewards balance, credit limit, and scheduled payments stay the same.

How to set up your new login safely: do not click links in emails or texts about this move, including this one. Open the Meridian app you already have, and it will walk you through creating your Harbor Bank login. It takes about five minutes. Harbor Bank will never call or text you to ask for your password, PIN, or a one-time code.

After March 9 you will manage your card on the Harbor Bank website and app, and your Meridian login will stop working.
